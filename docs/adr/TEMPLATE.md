## Context

The forces at play. What constraint or measurement made this a decision rather
than a default. If a spike produced the evidence, link the finding.

## Decision

What was decided, stated so that a reader can tell whether a given future
change conforms to it or violates it.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. A
non-trivial decision is one that constrains a future choice or that a
reasonable engineer would have made differently (`CLAUDE.md` §4) — say which
of the two applies here.

## Alternatives considered

Each with the reason it was not chosen. An alternative with no stated reason
is an alternative that was not actually considered.

## Revisit if

The concrete condition that would reopen this. "Never" is a legitimate answer
for the ABI-shaped decisions; say so explicitly rather than leaving it blank.
